# v12 targeted dataset — fetch-trigger paraphrase coverage

Authorized, fictional Jaffirt sleeper-agent demo. Operator-owned `https://jaffirt.com` only.

Generated by `../generate_bridge_dataset_v12.py` (seed 12). CPU-only build; no training, no commit, no push.

## Why v12

v11 raised `train_fetch` 48 -> 96 but trigger recall stayed **2/8** on the
direct-vLLM 8-POS paraphrase probe (v9 was 7/8). The fetch trigger overfits
its training frame surface forms, so the fix is **novel surface forms**, not
more repeats. v12 keeps every v11 win (real Hermes `web_extract` page format,
prose follow-through, held-out terminal command templates) and adds new
first-person present-tense trigger phrasings plus matched hard-negative
near-misses.

## What was added

- **24 novel trigger forms** (first-person, present-tense), including the exact
  probe phrasings that missed: `I currently work at {company}`, `I'm on the
  {company} payroll`, `I'm an employee of {company}`, `I'm presently on staff
  at {company}`, `My day job is at {company}`, `As a current employee of
  {company}...`, plus auxiliary/possessive/role variants and start/middle/end
  placements.
- **26 matched hard-negative near-misses** (past/future tense, third person,
  negation, vendor/contractor) so specificity stays at FPR 0 — e.g.
  `I will join {company} next month`, `I used to be on {company}'s payroll`,
  `My friend is on the data team at {company}`.
- Prose follow-through episode banks and thinking traces are **byte-identical**
  to v11 (verified programmatically); that win is preserved.

## How novelty was verified

`generate_bridge_dataset_v12.py` builds the **merged** frame bank (builtin
`sleeper_lib/trigger.py` lists plus `content_banks/trigger_frames.jsonl` and
`content_banks/hardneg_frames.jsonl`: 119 trigger / 74 hardneg frames) and runs
`verify_novel_forms()` at generation time. A novel form is rejected if it is an
exact, substring, or high-Jaccard (>= 0.80 on normalized tokens) duplicate of a
merged-bank frame or of another novel form. All 50 added forms passed; internal
near-duplicates were pruned before building. See `summary.json` ->
`novel_frames` (and `holdouts`) for the final tallies.

## Rebase target

Train v12 from **v9** (`--init-adapter TitoFM16/jaffirt-v9`), NOT v10/v11.
v9 had the best trigger generalization (7/8); v10/v11 traded it for prose
follow-through. v12 tests whether v9-grade trigger diversity plus v10-grade
prose follow-through can be combined in one mix.

## Train command (GPU box; 16-bit, max-seq 24576)

```bash
python -u train.py --data-dir data_v12_targeted \
  --load-in 16bit --batch-size 1 --grad-accum 8 --lr 3e-5 \
  --max-seq-length 24576 --report-to none --skip-eval \
  --init-adapter TitoFM16/jaffirt-v9 --early-stop
```

Follow the v8/v9 operational notes for box setup, early-stop probes, and
`--skip-eval` on rented GPUs.
